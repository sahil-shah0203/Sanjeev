# Evaluation plan

The application implements the learning rules in the root `01` guide. It does not claim improved exam scores, clinical competence, or a validated mastery probability.

## Before a medical pilot

- A qualified reviewer checks source support, current medical correctness, ambiguity, defensible answer/rubric, distractor rejection rationales, and suitability for the chosen cognitive task.
- Audit the entire initial pilot item set. Source grounding alone is insufficient: a quoted source can be outdated or wrong.
- Exercise failure modes: negation, units, temporal qualifiers, risk versus cause, multiple plausible answers, missing context, answer leakage, and model abstention.
- A source revision/report quarantines dependent items. Test that old attempt versions remain available and no alternate activity changes an original-card schedule.

## Research question

Does bounded, reviewed practice improve delayed retention or transfer per study minute compared with ordinary recall, without unacceptable workload or ambiguity? More activity and longer sessions are not themselves evidence of better learning.

Use opt-in participants, a prespecified comparison, and held-out delayed assessments. Randomize at an appropriate concept/learner cluster to reduce contamination. Keep the original scheduler and source content comparable. Do not use in-session correction as the primary delayed-retention endpoint.

## Outcomes

Primary candidates: delayed original recall and transfer to unseen, reviewed items on later days, with active study minutes as a cost. Track uncertainty, disputes, content-error reports, attrition, fatigue and time spent on interventions. Separate recall, discrimination, explanation and application observations; no unsupported universal score.

The current UI shows operational review counts, time, delayed-success days, and separate alternate-task observation counts. Raw review/exposure/attempt/session records can be exported for a consented analysis. Evaluation consent currently stores a preference only; there is no silent experiment enrollment or research upload.

## Defaults to test

One optional check per ten reviews, no more than 15% of a timed session, one brief explanation, a 30-minute exposure-contamination window, conservative self-check/abstention, and high-backlog suppression are hypotheses. Review burden, omission effects, time-cap overshoot on unusually slow responses, and the cost of conflict-induced repeated review must be measured. Do not optimize these parameters from the supplied three-card sample.

Publish the protocol, stopping criteria, content-error escalation route, and analysis plan before collecting outcomes. Release medical practice only after qualified review and reliability gates pass; broaden task formats based on observed benefit.
